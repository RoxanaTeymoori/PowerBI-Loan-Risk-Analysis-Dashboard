# Power BI Loan Risk & Fraud Analytics Dashboard
This project presents an end-to-end Power BI dashboard designed to analyze loan applications, assess credit risk, and detect potential fraud patterns using KPI-driven insights.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor loan performance, identify high-risk customers, analyze fraud trends across regions, and support data-driven credit decision-making.

## Tools & Skills

- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (Advanced Measures & KPIs)
- Data Modeling (Star Schema)
- KPI Design & Business Metrics
- Credit Risk & Fraud Analysis

## Dashboard Pages

- **Executive Summary** – High-level overview of loan performance and risk indicators
- **Credit Risk Analysis** – Credit score distribution, defaults, and risk segmentation
- **Fraud Detection** – Fraud trends by region, customer type, and loan purpose
- **Customer Overview** – Customer demographics, behavior, and risk profile
